✅ Privacy Policy for ClipShot Record Effective date: December 19, 2025 This Privacy Policy explains how ClipShot Record ("ClipShot," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the ClipShot Record mobile application (the "App"). The App is designed to work primarily offline and keeps your recordings and most settings on your device. 1. Information We Collect We do not collect personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports. The App does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and it does not transmit your recordings or personal data to our servers. Information stored locally on your device may include: Recordings you create (video and audio). App preferences (camera selection, max duration, storage mode, language, and similar settings). App lock credentials (stored as a salted, hashed value, not in plain text). A local Pro entitlement flag indicating whether Pro features are enabled. For paid features, the App may communicate with Google Play services to verify purchase or subscription status. This verification is handled by Google Play and does not involve our own servers. 2. Permissions and Device Access The App requests the following permissions only to provide core functionality: Camera: to capture video. Microphone: to capture audio with video. Foreground service (camera/microphone): to record while the App runs in the background. Notifications: to show recording status and controls. Vibration: to provide haptic feedback. Internet: to verify Pro purchases and subscriptions through Google Play services. You can manage permissions at any time in your device settings. If you deny permissions, some features may not work. 3. How We Use Information On-device information is used solely to: Record and save your clips. Manage hidden and visible recordings. Provide secure access with app lock and optional biometric unlock. Remember your preferences and settings. Verify Pro feature access through Google Play when applicable. 4. Storage and Security ClipShot Record stores data locally on your device: Hidden recordings are encrypted and stored in app-private storage. Visible recordings are saved to your device gallery in a "ClipShotRecord" folder. Temporary files may be created during recording, playback, or export and are removed when no longer needed. If you enable biometric unlock, authentication is handled by your device. We do not access or store your biometric data. 5. Sharing and Disclosure We do not sell, rent, or share your data. Your recordings remain on your device unless you choose to export, share, or move them using your device features. Exported recordings saved to your gallery may be accessible to other apps with gallery access or when you explicitly share them. Purchase and subscription processing is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details or financial information. 6. Backups Depending on your device and system settings, Android may back up app data (including preferences and hidden recordings) to your cloud backup provider. We do not control these backups. You can review or disable device backups in your system settings. 7. Data Retention and Deletion You control your data. You can delete recordings from within the App or from your device gallery. If you uninstall the App, app-private data is removed. Recordings saved to your gallery may remain unless you delete them manually. 8. Children's Privacy The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. 9. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date above and make the revised policy available within the App or at the published policy URL. 10.
